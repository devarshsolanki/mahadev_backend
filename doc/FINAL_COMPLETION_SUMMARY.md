## 🔄 Complete Order Lifecycle

```
1. Browse Products
   ↓
2. Add to Cart
   ↓
3. Apply Coupon (optional)
   ↓
4. Add Money to Wallet (if needed)
   ↓
5. Checkout
   ↓
6. Select Payment Method
   ↓
7. Order Created
   ↓
8. Stock Deducted
   ↓
9. Payment Processed
   ↓
10. Order Confirmed
    ↓
11. Assigned to Delivery Partner
    ↓
12. Out for Delivery
    ↓
13. Delivered
    
OR Cancel → Auto-Refund → Stock Restored
```

## 🔄 Complete Subscription Lifecycle

```
1. Create Subscription
   ↓
2. System Monitors (Cron Job)
   ↓
3. Due Date Arrives
   ↓
4. Auto Order Creation
   ↓
5. Wallet Payment
   ↓
6. Stock Deduction
   ↓
7. Next Delivery Scheduled
   ↓
8. Repeat

User can Pause/Resume/Cancel anytime
Auto-Pause after 3 consecutive failures
```
